Getting started

Pulze Mazz Editor controls a Hotone Pulze Mini from your PC over Bluetooth: you edit tones, save them, organize them into Songlists, make backups, and you can have an AI put a sound together for you.

What you need

  • A PC with Bluetooth turned on.
  • Chrome or Edge: they are the browsers that can talk to Bluetooth. Firefox and Safari cannot.
  • The Pulze Mini switched on. The editor has been tested with firmware 1.1.2.

Connecting

  1. Close the Pulze Editor app on your phone (or turn off the phone's Bluetooth). The Mini accepts one connection at a time: if the phone is connected, the PC won't find it.
  2. Press Connect the Pulze Mini in the middle, or Connect at the top right.
  3. In the browser window choose the Pulze Mini and press Pair.
  4. The editor reads the current tone and the list of tones. In a few seconds it's ready, and the light at the top right turns green.

To disconnect press Disconnect (or, in a narrow window, the green light). If the Jam is playing, it stops it first.

Changes go to the Mini as you make them: you hear the result right away. But they are not saved until you press Save…: switching tone loses them. See Saving, Undo and A/B.

Installing it as an app

From the website (mazzrelaz.github.io/PulzeMazzEditor) the editor can be installed on your PC: the strip “Install Pulze Mazz Editor on this PC” appears by itself at the bottom left; press Install and confirm. If you closed it, there is → Install on this PC (or the “Install” icon in Chrome's and Edge's address bar).

Once installed, it opens from the Desktop or the Start menu, in its own window without the browser bar, and it works even without internet (you only need Bluetooth and the Mini; Create AI preset and Dropbox need internet). When there is a new version, the editor tells you with a strip and an Update button.

Language

The editor starts in the language of your browser (Italian or English). To change it: → Italiano / English. The page reloads.

The screen

  • Top bar: reload (re-reads everything from the Mini), Vol (the tone's volume), BPM (the tone's tempo, used by delays with Sync on), the Jam, Connect and the menu (Backup, Settings, Guide, Language, Credits).
  • Below the bar: on the left Save…, Undo / Redo, A/B and the .prst files; in the middle the tone name with the arrows to the previous or next tone (next to it, in amber, “modified · not saved” when you have changed something); on the right Create AI preset… and Songlist.
  • In the middle, the stage: the block you chose, big, with its knobs and the list of models.
  • On the right, the list of tones: see Tones.
  • At the bottom, the chain: the seven blocks in the order the sound goes through them, from IN to OUT.
  • Messages appear at the bottom right: green when something worked (they go away by themselves), red when something is wrong (they stay until you press the ✕), grey with a spinning circle while the editor is working.

In a narrow window (a small laptop, or half the screen) the page gets more compact: the Jam becomes a button that opens its own panel, some buttons show only their icon and the tone name moves to its own row.

The chain and the blocks

The seven blocks are DYN (dynamics: gate, compressor), PRE (effects before the amp), AMP, CAB (cabinet), EQ/MOD (equalizers and modulations), FX1 and FX2 (delays, reverbs and more).

  • Turning a block on and off: click the colored label above the picture (blue = on). A block that is off is grey.
  • Showing it on the stage: click the picture.
  • Changing model: from the drop-down under the picture (models are grouped by category), or from the list to the right of the stage, which also has a search box. The new model starts with its factory values.
  • Moving a block in the chain: grab the picture and drag it where you want; the others make room.

Auto CAB

On the AMP stage there is the Auto CAB switch. It is a setting of the Mini, not of the tone: it applies to all tones. When it's on and you change amp, the Mini picks the matching cabinet by itself.

Sound Clones and user IRs

If you have loaded Sound Clones or IRs on the Mini, they show up among the models with the name you gave them. On the stage, under the title, there is Rename… to change it. Loading new ones from the PC is not possible: you need the phone app.

The knobs

  • Turning: click the knob and drag up (more) or down (less).
  • More precisely: hold Shift while dragging.
  • Mouse wheel over the knob: one step at a time.
  • Double-click: back to the factory value.
  • Selectors with few choices are buttons side by side (for example Cool / Hot); those with many choices are a drop-down; switches are On / Off pills.
  • Delay and modulation times have the Sync pill: when it's on, the time is a division (1/4, dotted 1/8…) of the tone's BPM; when it's off, it's in milliseconds or Hertz.

Saving, Undo and A/B

Save…

Save… opens the save window: choose the slot (the current tone's slot is suggested) and the name (16 characters max, no accented letters). You can save it to a different slot: if that slot is full, what was there is overwritten, but the editor first makes a safety copy of it.

Undo and Redo

The curved arrows next to Save go back and forward through your changes (knobs, models, on/off, chain, even a whole import). They work as long as you stay on the same tone.

A/B: the comparison

After changing something, A/B lets you hear the tone as it is saved (the button turns amber). Press it again to go back to your changes. That way you can hear whether the change is really an improvement.

Tones

The Mini has 200 slots. The list on the right shows the ones that are full.

  • Click a tone: it plays. If you have unsaved changes, it asks you first.
  • Search tones…: type part of the name or the slot number.
  • The drop-down at the top chooses what to show: All tones or a Songlist.
  • In “All tones”, the small list icon next to a name means that tone is in at least one Songlist; hover over it and it tells you which.
  • Right-click a tone (or the that appears when you hover over it): Play, Rename…, Copy to another slot…, Swap with…, Delete….
The Mini can't copy a tone directly: the editor plays it and saves it to the new slot. A swap is three copies through an empty spare slot. Songlists point to slots: after a swap, a Songlist that contained slot 5 will play the tone that is now in slot 5. The editor reminds you before doing it.

.prst files: import and export

A .prst is a tone file of the Pulze Editor phone app: you can share it, download it from the internet, keep it on your PC.

  • Export (down arrow): saves the current tone to a .prst in the Downloads folder. The phone app can import it.
  • Import… (folder): choose a .prst. Before touching the Mini the editor shows what is in the file: the chain, the models, what is on and what is off.

When you press Import the file goes into the current tone, replacing what was there, and the editor re-reads the blocks to check. It is not saved: listen to it, tweak it, and if you like it press Save… (the suggested name is the file's). If you don't like it, Undo removes it, or just switch tone without saving.

If a block in the file uses a model the Mini can't put in that slot, the editor says so (“the tone's own block stays”) and keeps the block that was there.

Create AI preset

Describe the sound you want in words (“AC/DC's crunch”, “80s clean with chorus and a long delay”) and an artificial intelligence builds it using the Mini's real models: it picks the amp, cabinet and effects, puts them in order, decides what is on and sets the knobs. You see the result before it goes on the Mini, and it arrives in the current tone like a .prst file: nothing is saved by itself.

What you need: an API key

The editor has no AI of its own: it uses OpenAI's (ChatGPT) or Anthropic's (Claude), with your own API key. The key is a kind of password that lets the editor send requests on your behalf.

API keys are pay-as-you-go, billed separately: a ChatGPT Plus or Claude Pro subscription does not include them. Each preset costs a few cents (it depends on the model you choose). On the provider's site you can add a small amount of credit and set a spending limit.

Your key is safe

  • It goes directly from your PC to OpenAI or Anthropic, and to nobody else. There is no editor server in between: the editor is a page running on your computer.
  • The author of the editor never sees or receives it, and there is no editor account it could end up in.
  • It stays only in your browser, on this PC, so you don't have to type it every time. It doesn't go to the Mini, to Dropbox, into backups or into .prst files.
  • You can always delete it on the provider's site (on the API keys page): from then on it no longer works, for anyone. And with the spending limit you always know the most you can spend.

Getting an OpenAI key

  1. Go to platform.openai.com and sign in (or create an account).
  2. Under Billing, add some credit (a few dollars is enough).
  3. Under API keys press Create new secret key, give it a name (for example “Pulze”) and copy the key: it starts with sk-. It is shown only once: copy it right away.

Getting an Anthropic (Claude) key

  1. Go to console.anthropic.com and sign in (or create an account).
  2. Under Billing, add some credit.
  3. Under API Keys press Create Key, give it a name and copy the key: it starts with sk-ant-.

The first time

  1. Press Create AI preset… (purple, top right).
  2. In the The AI box choose OpenAI or Claude (Anthropic).
  3. Paste the key into the key field. The editor tries it straight away: if it works, the Model drop-down fills with the models on your account. If it doesn't, the reason is written underneath (wrong key, no credit left…).
  4. Choose the model. The first in the list is the newest and usually the best; “mini” or “nano” models cost less but make more mistakes. With Claude the default is Claude Opus 5.5.

Key and model stay in this browser: next time you don't need to enter them again. To change the key there is Key… next to the model. You can keep both the OpenAI and the Claude key and switch between them.

Creating a sound

  1. In the The sound box, write what you want. Any language works.
  2. If you want to improve the tone that is playing rather than start from scratch, tick Start from the current tone: the AI also receives the current tone and modifies it (“more gain”, “remove the chorus and add a spring reverb”).
  3. Press Create. Underneath you see “…is composing the sound” with the seconds ticking: it usually takes from 10 seconds to a couple of minutes, depending on the model. Cancel stops it.
  4. The Try “name” window appears: the proposed chain, block by block, and underneath the AI's explanation (why it chose those models). If something doesn't fit, it's written there.
  5. Press Import: the sound goes into the current tone (the previous one is replaced, but not on the Mini: it isn't saved).
  6. Play and listen. Adjust the knobs by ear: the AI can't hear your guitar or your amp.
  7. Like it? Save…, choose the slot (better an empty one, so you don't lose the previous tone) and the name. Don't like it? Undo, or try again with a different description.

Writing a good description

  • Genre and era: “60s blues”, “modern metal”, “clean funk”.
  • An artist or a song: “the Comfortably Numb solo”, “the Back in Black rhythm”. The AI knows the gear of many famous guitarists and picks the closest models.
  • The effects you want (or don't): “with a tempo-synced delay, no reverb”.
  • How much gain: clean, crunch, distorted, very distorted.
  • How you'll use it: “for quiet playing at home on headphones”, “for rhythm”, “for solos”.
  • The guitar, if it matters: “with a single-coil Strat”, “for humbuckers”.

Examples

“Malcolm Young crunch: 70s Marshall, low gain, no effects, a touch of reverb.”

“Sparkling clean for arpeggios, light chorus and a dotted-eighth delay, The Edge style.”

“Warm, singing lead for blues solos, Gary Moore style, with a short delay.”

With “Start from the current tone”: “Same sound but less bass and more presence, and turn off the chorus.”

Good to know

  • The AI only uses models the Mini really has, plus your loaded Sound Clones and IRs, with the names you gave them.
  • Each request is independent: if the result isn't right, rephrase rather than repeat the same words.
  • If the AI puts a model in a slot where the Mini doesn't accept it, the preview says “the tone's own block stays”: that block doesn't change.
  • What leaves your PC: the description you write, the model catalog and, with “Start from the current tone”, the current tone. It goes to the provider you chose (OpenAI or Anthropic) with your key, and to nobody else. The key stays only in your browser (see Your key is safe).

If it doesn't work

  • “The key does not work”: copy the whole key again, without spaces; check that there is credit on the site.
  • “It did not work: …” with a message from the provider (too many requests, credit used up, model not available): try another model or try again in a moment.
  • “Claude refused the request”: it rarely happens; rephrase the description.
  • The button is grey: the editor is still reading the blocks from the Mini, or an import is in progress. Wait a moment.

Songlists

A Songlist is a set list: the tones in the order you use them for a gig or a genre. The Mini holds up to 40, each with up to 50 tones. Songlist opens the screen.

  • On the left are all the tones. Drag a tone onto a Songlist to add it at the end. Double-click: it plays. Hovering shows a pencil (rename) and ✕ (deletes the tone from the Mini).
  • On the right, one box per Songlist. Click a tone: it plays. Drag tones inside the box to change the order, or onto another box to add them there. The ✕ on a row removes it from the Songlist (the tone stays on the Mini).
  • Drag a box's header to move the Songlist. The pencil and ✕ in the header rename or delete it. New Songlist creates one.
Songlist changes are a draft: changed boxes have an amber header and at the top it says “unsaved changes”. Nothing goes to the Mini until you press Save to the Mini, which does everything at once and then re-reads to check (with many changes it can take half a minute). Discard changes goes back to what is on the Mini. Before saving, the editor makes a safety copy of the Songlists.

Renaming and deleting a tone (in the left column), on the other hand, happens immediately.

The Jam

The Jam is the Mini's drum machine, to play along with. It's in the top bar (in a narrow window, press Jam and its panel opens).

  • starts and stops the drums; while they play, in a narrow window, the Jam button is blue.
  • The drop-down chooses the rhythm: 100 of them, grouped by genre (8-Beat, 16-Beat, Rock, Funk, Jazz, Blues, Latin… down to the metronomes). The arrows go to the previous or next rhythm.
  • The number is the drums' BPM; Tap sets it by tapping in time; Sync is the same drum Sync as in the app.
  • The slider is the drums' volume.

Settings and Global EQ

→ Settings opens the Mini's general settings, as in the app: they apply to all tones. Every change goes to the Mini immediately.

  • Sound Mode: the overall character of the output (Standard, Rock, Hi-Res…).
  • Audio, USB Audio, Tuner: levels and adjustments. The big knobs are dragged up and down or turned with the mouse wheel.
  • Display: the Mini's screen and the theme color.
  • Global EQ: an equalizer on everything, with its own on/off switch.

The Global EQ

The graph shows the curve. The points L (low cut), 1-4 (the four bands) and H (high cut) can be dragged: left and right changes the frequency, up and down the gain; the mouse wheel over a point changes its width (Q). Below are the tabs for each band with its ON, GAIN, FREQ and Q, and the overall volume.

The curve is drawn by the editor to show you the effect; it isn't measured by the Mini.

Backup

→ Backup…. Under “Backup…” the menu tells you when you made your last backup (in amber if it's more than 14 days ago).

On this PC

  • Full backup: all tones (one .prst each), the settings (.glb) and the Songlists (.json) in one .zip, in the Downloads folder. With many tones it takes a couple of minutes: let it work.
  • Tone → Restore presets…: puts tones back from a backup .zip. See Restoring tones.
  • Settings → Export / Import…: only the general settings, in a .glb file (the same as the app's).
  • Songlist → Export / Import…: only the Songlists. Import also accepts the full backup .zip. Songlists are tied to tone slots: if a slot now holds a different tone, it tells you first.

In the Dropbox box there is the same backup, sent to your Dropbox.

Restoring tones from a backup

From Backup… → Restore presets… (choose a .zip on your PC) or from Dropbox → Restore: Tone (the .zip chosen in the list). The Restore tones from a backup window opens.

  1. The editor compares each tone in the backup with what is now in the same slot on the Mini (it takes about twenty seconds) and writes next to each one: identical, different (and how), empty slot.
  2. Only the different and empty ones are already ticked. You can change that by hand, or use Only the different ones, All, None.
  3. Press Restore. For each ticked tone the editor puts it in the current tone, saves it to its slot and re-reads it to check. A few seconds per tone. Stop finishes the one in progress and stops.
  4. At the end it goes back to the tone you started from.
The work goes through the current tone: if you have unsaved changes they are lost (it asks you). Overwritten tones first go into a safety copy.

Backup to Dropbox

Backups can also go to your Dropbox, so they are safe if the PC breaks. The editor doesn't go through any server of its own: it talks directly to Dropbox, through a small Dropbox “app” that you create on your account. You do it once, and it's simpler than it sounds.

1. Create your Dropbox app (once)

  1. Go to dropbox.com/developers/apps and sign in with your Dropbox account.
  2. Press Create app.
  3. Choose Scoped access, then App folder (the app only sees its own folder, not the rest of your Dropbox).
  4. Give it any name, for example “Pulze Mazz Editor” plus something of yours (the name must be unique across all of Dropbox), and press Create app.
  5. Open the Permissions tab, tick files.content.write and files.content.read and press Submit at the bottom of the page.
  6. Go back to the Settings tab and copy the App key (a string of letters and numbers). The App secret is not needed.

2. Connect the editor

  1. In the editor: → Backup…, Dropbox box.
  2. Paste the App key into the field and press Connect. The Dropbox page opens (if it doesn't, use the “open Dropbox” link).
  3. On Dropbox press Continue and Allow: it shows you a code. Copy it.
  4. Back in the editor, paste the code into the second field and press Done.

The connection stays in this browser: next time the Dropbox box is already ready. Pressing “Connect” again halfway through does no harm. A code works only once.

3. Using it

  • Backup to Dropbox: the same .zip as the full backup, in Dropbox / Apps / (your app's name) / Pulze Mini. Each backup is a new file: nothing is overwritten.
  • The Backup drop-down shows the backups on Dropbox, newest first.
  • Restore: Tone (see Restoring tones), Settings or Songlist from the chosen backup.
  • On this PC → Download: downloads the chosen backup to the Downloads folder.
  • Dropbox → Disconnect: removes the connection from this browser (the files on Dropbox stay).

Safety copies

Before any job that overwrites something on the Mini, the editor keeps by itself a copy of how it was: saving over a tone in another slot, copying or swapping tones, deleting a tone, importing settings, saving Songlists, restoring tones from a backup.

You'll find them in Backup… → Safety copies (click to open the list): when, what and why. For each one, Restore puts it back and Download saves it to your PC.

Safety copies live in this browser (the last 40), not on the Mini and not on Dropbox: if you clear the browser's data or change PC, they are gone. They are a parachute, not a backup: still make a Full backup now and then.

If something goes wrong

  • The Mini doesn't appear in the Bluetooth window: close the app on your phone (or turn off its Bluetooth), check that the Mini is on and try again. If still nothing, switch the Mini off and on again.
  • “This browser has no Bluetooth for web pages”: open the editor with Chrome or Edge.
  • A red message says the Mini did not confirm: press (reload) to see how the tone really is on the Mini.
  • The connection dropped: press Connect again. Unsaved changes stay on the Mini until you switch tone.
  • I ruined a tone: if you haven't saved it, switch tone or press Undo. If you have saved it, look in the safety copies or restore it from a backup.
